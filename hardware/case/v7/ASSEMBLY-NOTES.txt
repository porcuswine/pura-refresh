PURA ELECTRONICS CASE v4 — SENSOR OPENING AND SPLIT PLUG KEEPER

Changes
1. Radial 8 mm opening at 7 oclock in the base wall. Orientation matches the
   board photo: look into the open case with the LED arc at 12 oclock. This is
   for the clear protruding component beside D20 and left of the pushbutton.
   Opening center is 15.25 mm above the rear exterior, at PCB mid-thickness.
   Bore diameter/height are estimates; check that the sensor clears and sees
   outside through the hole without loading its leads.
2. The closed plug keeper is replaced by two halves that approach from opposite
   sides around the narrow plug section. Nothing needs to pass over the larger
   flange. Two screws retain each half (four M2 x 6 mm screws in total).
3. Base has four keeper screw bosses instead of three. Print the new base with
   the new halves; the halves do not match the v3 base screw layout.

Print
base-TEST-v4.stl: flat back on plate, open tray upward.
plug-keeper-upper-v4.stl and plug-keeper-lower-v4.stl: both flat, as supplied.
The v3 lid is reusable. lid-TEST-v4.stl is included and has identical geometry.
Use the same dimensional settings as successful gauges. 0.20 mm layers,
3+ walls, 4+ top/bottom layers are reasonable fit-test starting settings.
Check slicer layer preview at the sensor bore; no supports are intended.

Assembly, unplugged
Fit original plug into base recess (confirmed 30.04 mm flange recess; estimated
25.90 mm neck opening). Slide keeper halves around neck from opposite sides,
seat on bosses, and fasten gently. A 0.30 mm seam separates the halves.
Case screws: two M3 x 10 mm into 2.5 mm printed pilots.
PCB screws: proposed M2.5 x 6 mm with insulating washers into 2 mm pilots;
check washer clearance and screw engagement before installing.
Board mounts retain confirmed gauge dimensions: 42.80 mm spacing, 5.30 mm
locating pegs, 1.70 mm peg height, 8 mm shoulders and 12 mm floor clearance.
Seat board, verify sensor passes freely through the 7 oclock wall opening,
and confirm lid closes without squeezing components or wires.

Remaining assumptions
Plug center remains 26 mm below PCB center. Sensor exact height/diameter,
board hole-row offset and complete component envelopes are not measured.
The pushbutton and front LED access are still not modeled. The original plug
rotation mechanism and retention strength are not validated by this test.
Body 92.27 mm diameter, 107.6 mm across screw tabs, assembled depth 34.8 mm.
All four STLs pass watertight single-solid checks.

Unpowered mechanical test only. Do not connect this prototype to mains.
Electrical insulation, enclosure openings/access, material, temperature and
plug retention require assessment before powered use.
